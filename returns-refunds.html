How It Works

Four simple steps.

Most returns are processed start-to-finish in under 10 days.

Start Online

Visit our Returns Center at glaex.com/returns and enter your order number plus email.

Print Label

Select items, choose refund or store credit, and we'll email a prepaid USPS label within minutes.

Ship Back

Pack items in original or sturdy packaging, attach the label, and drop off at any USPS location.

Get Refunded

Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5 business days of us receiving the return.

Return Window

You have 14 days from the delivery date to start a return. The 14-day window is calculated from the date your package was marked "Delivered" by the carrier.

Return Conditions

To qualify for a return, items must be:

  • Unworn, unwashed, and in original condition.
  • With all original tags attached.
  • Free from stains, makeup, deodorant, perfume, or pet hair.
  • Packaged securely to prevent damage in transit.

We inspect every return upon receipt. Items not meeting these conditions may be refused or subject to a restocking fee.

Final Sale Items

The following items are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged:

  • Items purchased at 40% off or more.
  • Intimates, bodysuits, and shapewear.
  • Swimwear (if hygienic liner is removed or compromised).
  • Earrings and pierced jewelry.
  • Beauty and personal care items.
  • Gift cards.
  • Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll send you an email to confirm. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5 business days.

Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take an additional 3-10 business days for the refund to appear on your statement.

Original shipping costs are non-refundable Shipping fees you paid at checkout are not refunded unless the return is due to our error (wrong item, defective product).

Store Credit

Prefer store credit? You'll receive 110% of the item value as a credit (10% bonus). Store credit doesn't expire and can be combined with future promotions.

Exchanges

We process exchanges as a return + new order. To exchange for a different size or color, start a return online and place a new order for the item you want. This ensures the replacement ships immediately while we process your return.

Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrives damaged or defective, contact us within 7 days of delivery at hello@glaex.com with photos. We'll replace, refund, or store-credit immediately — and cover all shipping costs.

Wrong Item Received

If we shipped the wrong item, we sincerely apologize. Contact us within 7 days at hello@glaex.com and we'll send the correct piece at no charge plus a prepaid return label for the incorrect one.

International Returns

International customers are responsible for return shipping costs and any customs/duties fees. Please use a tracked service — we cannot refund items lost in transit without proof of delivery.

Late or Missing Refunds

If your refund hasn't arrived after 10 business days:

  • Check your bank account once more.
  • Contact your credit card company — processing can take time.
  • Email us at hello@glaex.com with your order number.
