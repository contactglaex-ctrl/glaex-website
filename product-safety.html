vi.

We require safety documentation from every supplier and conduct annual audits of high-volume partners.

How to Wear Your GLAEX Pieces Safely

Most clothing safety issues come down to care and use. To keep your pieces in great condition — and avoid skin irritation or fabric damage — follow the care label inside each garment.

  • Wash before first wear. This removes residual dyes, finishes, and packaging materials.
  • Follow care instructions. Machine wash, hand wash, or dry clean as labeled. Ignoring labels can cause shrinkage, color bleeding, or fabric degradation.
  • Avoid prolonged direct sunlight. Many fabrics, especially dyed natural fibers, fade with extended UV exposure.
  • Store properly. Hang structured pieces; fold knits to preserve shape.

Allergy & Sensitivity Notice

Some customers may have skin sensitivities to natural or synthetic fibers, dyes, or fabric treatments. If you have known sensitivities:

  • Always wash items before first wear.
  • Avoid wearing irritating items against bare skin.
  • Discontinue wear if you experience skin irritation, redness, or itching.

If you experience an allergic reaction to a GLAEX product, please discontinue use immediately and contact us at safety@glaex.com with the order number and product details. We take these reports seriously and may issue a full refund.

Defective Products & Recalls

In the unlikely event a product is recalled, we will:

  • Notify affected customers directly by email within 48 hours of the recall.
  • Provide clear instructions on how to return the item and receive a full refund.
  • Cover all return shipping costs for recalled items.
  • Post recall notices prominently on our website and Instagram.

If you believe you've received a defective product, contact us at safety@glaex.com with photos. We respond within 24 hours and will replace, refund, or store-credit at our cost.

Reporting a Safety Concern

If you experience or observe a safety issue with a GLAEX product:

  • Stop wearing the item immediately.
  • Preserve the item and packaging where possible.
  • Email safety@glaex.com with order number, product name, photos, and a description.
  • For urgent or serious safety issues, call us at +1 (800) 555-0150.

You may also report safety incidents directly to the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission at saferproducts.gov.

EU & UK Compliance

For customers in the European Union and United Kingdom, our products comply with applicable EU REACH regulation, EU GPSR (General Product Safety Regulation), and UK product safety requirements. Authorized Representative information available on request at safety@glaex.com.
