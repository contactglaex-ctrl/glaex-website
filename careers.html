Why GLAEX

A small team. Big ambition.

GLAEX is an early-stage modern boutique with a long-term vision. We're not chasing exits, we're not optimizing for vanity metrics, and we're definitely not interested in being the next "fast fashion" brand. We're building something that gets better with every season — and we want people on this team who care about that as much as we do.

Working here means being closer to the work than you've ever been. No layers. No politics. No twelve-step approval chains for a single product photo. You'll have ownership, autonomy, and the freedom to do your best work — alongside teammates who push you to do even better.

We don't promise free snacks or ping-pong tables. We promise meaningful work, mutual respect, and the kind of creative environment most people leave their corporate jobs to find.

Our Culture

How we work together.

Six principles that shape how we hire, how we collaborate, and how we treat each other every day.

i.

Ownership Over Permission

We hire smart people and trust them to make decisions. If you need to ask permission for everything, you'll be miserable here.

ii.

Craft Over Speed

We move fast — but never at the expense of quality. We'd rather ship one beautiful thing than ten mediocre ones.

iii.

Direct, Always Kind

Honest feedback is how we grow. Delivered with care is how we stay a team.

iv.

Customer-Obsessed

Every decision starts with her. If it doesn't make the experience better for our customer, we don't ship it.

v.

Bias Toward Action

Strong opinions, loosely held. Try things, learn fast, iterate. Done is better than perfect — but excellent beats both.

vi.

Real Lives, Real Boundaries

We work hard during work hours. Then we close the laptop. We respect your time, your energy, and your life outside this team.

What We're Really Hiring For

We don't hire for résumés.
We hire for taste.

"Skills can be taught. Tools can be learned. Taste — the ability to know what's good, why it's good, and how to make it better — is what separates the people who build great brands from the people who just work at them."

We care less about where you went to school and more about what you've made. Less about your title at your last company and more about the work you're proudest of. If you have a portfolio, a side project, a Substack, a Pinterest board you've curated for years — we want to see it.

Bring us your taste. We'll handle the rest.

Hiring Process

Four steps. No mystery.

Most hires move from application to offer in 2-3 weeks. We respect your time as much as our own.

Application

Send us your résumé, portfolio, or anything you've made you're proud of. Skip the cover letter — tell us what you'd build here instead.

Intro Call

30 minutes with the hiring manager. Conversational, two-way. We're learning about each other, not running a checklist.

Work Sample

A small, paid project or live work session — designed to mirror what you'd actually do here. Never more than 4 hours of your time.

Team Meet

Meet 2-3 future teammates. Ask hard questions. Hear honest answers. Then we make an offer — usually within 48 hours.
