What to expect.

Tracking

You'll receive a tracking link by email once your order ships, and you can also find it in your account under Orders.

Tracking can take a day or two to start updating after dispatch — that's normal, and it doesn't mean anything is wrong.

ii

Packaging

Your order is packed to travel safely and arrive in good condition. We're a young brand — our packaging is practical and protective rather than elaborate, and we'd rather put the care into the products themselves.
