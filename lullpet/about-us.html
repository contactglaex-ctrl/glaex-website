About Lullpet

We started Lullpet for the pets who couldn't tell us they were hurting.

A small story about an anxious rescue, a market full of empty promises, and the calm we built instead.

It began on a Tuesday night in Brooklyn.

My rescue, Maya, had been pacing the apartment for three hours. She'd done it every night since I adopted her — pacing, panting, never settling. I tried everything I could find: training, supplements, even prescriptions. Nothing fully worked.

I went looking for help — really looking — and what I found was a market flooded with cheap, generic "calming" products that were mostly marketing. Hollow promises wrapped in fluffy faux fur.

So I started building what I wished existed.

Lullpet is calming essentials for cats and dogs, designed with veterinary input and tested in real homes with real anxious pets. No cheap fillers. No empty claims. Just thoughtful products that help pets rest — because every pet deserves a quiet day.

— Sarah, Founder Brooklyn, New York
