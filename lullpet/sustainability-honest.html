Sustainability

An honest word on sustainability.

We won't pretend to be perfectly green. We'd rather tell you exactly where a small, young brand actually stands — and where it doesn't.

Where we stand

Honest, not performative.

Sustainability has become a marketing checkbox for most pet brands — green leaf icons, vague "eco" language, recyclable claims with no infrastructure behind them. We've chosen not to do that.

Lullpet is a new, small brand. We don't run our own factories or shipping fleet, so we won't claim certifications we haven't earned or carbon figures we can't prove. What we can do is be deliberate about the choices that are genuinely ours to make.

"We'd rather earn a claim than print one."

Here's the honest version — what we actually do today, and what we genuinely want to get better at. No badges we haven't earned. No numbers we can't stand behind.

What we actually do

The choices that are genuinely ours.

i

A deliberately small range

We sell a focused set of calming essentials — not an endless catalogue. Fewer products, chosen to be used and kept, not impulse-bought and discarded.

ii

Built to be kept

We favour durable, washable, long-lasting pieces over disposable ones. The most sustainable product is simply the one you don't have to replace.

iii

A lean, online-only operation

No retail floors, no chain of our own warehouses. A small online brand starts with a smaller footprint — and we'd like to keep it that way as we grow.

iv

No greenwashing

We won't print a leaf on a box and call it eco. If we can't verify a claim ourselves, we don't make it — and we'd rather say "we're not there yet" than imply otherwise.

What we want to get better at

Honest intentions, not promises.

→
Know our supply chain better — so that, in time, we can speak about materials and sourcing with real knowledge rather than guesswork.
→
Reduce packaging waste — as we gain more control over fulfilment, we want to push toward lower-waste, plastic-minimal packaging.
→
Favour better-made products — as the range grows, we want durability and material quality to weigh as heavily as price.
→
Share real figures one day — when we have data worth publishing, we will. Until then, we won't invent any.
In short

We're not a perfectly green brand. We're an honest one.

This page will grow as we do. If we ever earn a real certification or a real number, you'll see it here — and not a day before.
