Pet Sizing Guide

Get the right fit, the first time.

A snug fit matters — especially for calming products. Use the guides below, or send us a photo and we'll help.

Calming Cuddler Bed

Bed sizing, by weight.

Measure your pet from nose to base of tail when stretched out. When you're between sizes, choose the next size up for comfort.

Size Inner diameter Pet weight Common breeds
S 18 inches 5–15 lbs Most cats, small dogs (chihuahuas, yorkies)
M 24 inches 15–35 lbs French bulldogs, beagles, terriers
L 30 inches 35–50 lbs Labradors, golden retrievers, pit mixes
When in doubt, size up. Pets love to curl with room to spare — and the raised rim only calms them if they can fully sink into it.
Anxiety Vest

Vest sizing, by chest girth.

Measure your dog's chest at the widest point — just behind the front legs. The gentle, even pressure is what calms them, so a snug fit is essential.

Size Chest girth Common breeds
XS12–16 inchesChihuahuas, toy breeds, small terriers
S16–22 inchesBeagles, dachshunds, small spaniels
M22–28 inchesBorder collies, cocker spaniels, bulldogs
L28–34 inchesLabradors, golden retrievers, boxers
XL34–40 inchesGerman shepherds, large mixes, mastiffs
Between sizes? Go smaller. The vest works through deep-pressure therapy — too loose, and it won't deliver the calming effect your dog needs.
