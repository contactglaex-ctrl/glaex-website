Pet sizing guide

Get the right fit, the first time.

A snug fit matters — especially for calming products. Use the guides below or email us a photo. We'll help.

Calming Cuddler Bed

Bed sizing by weight.

Measure your pet from nose to base of tail when stretched out. Pick the next size up for comfort.

SizeInner DiameterPet WeightCommon Breeds
S18"5–15 lbsMost cats, small dogs (chihuahuas, yorkies)
M24"15–35 lbsFrench bulldogs, beagles, terriers
L30"35–50 lbsLabs, golden retrievers, pit mixes
When in doubt — size up. Pets love to curl with room to spare. The raised rim only calms if they can fully sink into it.
Anxiety Vest

Vest sizing by chest girth.

Measure your dog's chest at the widest point — just behind the front legs. The gentle pressure is what calms them, so a snug fit is essential.

SizeChest GirthCommon Breeds
XS12–16"Chihuahuas, toy breeds, small terriers
S16–22"Beagles, dachshunds, small spaniels
M22–28"Border collies, cocker spaniels, bulldogs
L28–34"Labs, golden retrievers, boxers
XL34–40"German shepherds, large mixes, mastiffs
Between sizes? Go smaller. The vest works through deep-pressure therapy — too loose and it won't deliver the calming effect.
