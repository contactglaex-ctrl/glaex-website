i. Shipping & delivery

Getting to your door.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are fulfilled from within the United States and typically arrive in about 5–8 business days after they're processed. US shipping is free on orders over $50.

Do you ship internationally?

For now, we ship only within the United States. If we expand beyond that, we'll announce it on the site.

How do I track my order?

You'll receive a tracking link by email once your order ships. You can also find it in your account under Orders.

What if my order is late or missing?

Email hello@lullpet.com with your order number. We reply to every message within 24 hours, and we'll help track it down or sort out a replacement.

ii. Returns & refunds

The 30-day calm guarantee.

What's your return policy?

You have thirty days from delivery to return an item for a full refund — no restocking fees. Items only need to be in reasonable condition; we understand pets test things. Email hello@lullpet.com to start a return.

Do I pay for return shipping?

If an item arrives defective or damaged, we cover the return shipping. For change-of-mind returns, the return shipping cost is the customer's.

When will I get my refund?

We process refunds within 3 business days of receiving your return. Depending on your bank, the credit usually appears on your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

iii. Products & use

Built for real pets.

Will a calming product definitely work for my pet?

We can't promise that — and we won't. Every pet is different. Calming products can genuinely help by making a space more comfortable and a routine more settled, but they're a support, not a cure.

If your pet has severe, sudden, or worsening anxiety, please speak to a veterinarian. A product is never a substitute for proper veterinary care.

How long until I see a difference?

It varies. Some pets settle within days, others take longer, and some don't respond much at all. Give a new product a couple of weeks alongside a steady daily routine before deciding — and remember the 30-day guarantee is there if it isn't right.

Are your products safe for puppies and kittens?

Please check the age guidance in each product's description, since it varies by item. If you're ever unsure whether something suits a young, senior, or unwell pet, ask your vet — we'd always rather you double-check than guess.

Are pheromone diffusers safe around children and other animals?

Pheromones are species-specific and drug-free — for example, feline pheromones are only detected by cats, not by humans, dogs, or birds. As with any product, follow the instructions included with the item, and ask your vet if you have specific health concerns at home.

Can I wash the beds?

Most of our beds are designed to be washable, but washing instructions differ by product. Always follow the care label and the guidance on that product's page.

The brand behind it.

Where do you ship from?

How does the 1% to shelters work?

We pledge 1% of every order's revenue — not profit — to vetted animal shelters and rescues. It's a commitment built into how Lullpet is run. You can read more on our 1% pledge page.
