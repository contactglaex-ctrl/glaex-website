Our Pledge

Calm pets. Better world.

We pledge 1% of every order to vetted animal shelters and rescues. Not "up to". Not "sometimes". A fixed share of every single order — written into how Lullpet is run.

Why one percent

Because the calm we sell at home should reach pets without one.

The pets in our homes are lucky. They have beds, names, dinner waiting. Most of the pets in shelters had that too, once — and lost it through no fault of their own.

We can't fix the whole system. But we can take a fixed percentage of every order we ship and direct it, consistently, to the people doing the work on the ground.

"Not a marketing line. A fixed share of every order."

So we built it into the brand from the start: every order placed on lullpet.com sets aside 1% of revenue — not profit — for shelter and rescue work. It's a young commitment, and we'd rather tell you exactly how it works than dress it up with numbers we haven't earned yet.

The commitment

How the pledge works.

i

One percent, every order

A fixed 1% of revenue from every order is set aside for shelter and rescue work — automatically, with no minimum and no exceptions.

ii

Given regularly

The amount set aside is donated on a regular schedule to vetted, registered animal welfare organizations — not held back for a convenient moment.

iii

Reported openly

As the program grows, we'll share where the contributions go. We'd rather report real figures later than print invented ones today.

Lullpet is a new brand. This page describes a commitment we are building, honestly — not a history we haven't lived yet.

Who we support

How we choose where it goes.

We want every contribution to reach organizations doing genuine, accountable work for animals.

i

Registered & vetted

We support registered, non-profit animal shelters and rescues with a clear, verifiable record of welfare work.

ii

National and local

We aim for a mix — established national organizations alongside smaller local rescues that rarely get the spotlight.

iii

Nominated by customers

Know a shelter that deserves support? Customers can nominate one, and we genuinely read every suggestion.

Transparency

We'll show our work — once there's work to show.

Rather than publish a polished impact report on day one, we'd rather earn one. As orders come in and contributions are made, we'll share what's been given and where. Honest numbers, when they're real.
