How it began

It started with a simple frustration.

Anyone who has lived with an anxious pet knows the feeling — the pacing, the hiding, the restless nights, the sense that your cat or dog is struggling and can't tell you why.

When we went looking for help, we found a market crowded with cheap, generic "calming" products and a lot of empty marketing. Plenty of promises. Very little care.

So we set out to build something better.

Lullpet is a focused collection of calming essentials for cats and dogs — chosen and designed around how pets actually settle: enclosure, soft texture, warmth, and routine. No filler. No hollow claims. Just thoughtful products, honest information, and a brand that treats your pet's calm as seriously as you do.

Modern life is loud, and anxious pets feel it. We make essentials that bring stillness — beautiful enough to leave out, soft enough to sink into.
