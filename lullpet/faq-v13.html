i. Shipping & delivery

Getting to your door.

How fast do you ship?

Orders ship within 24 hours from our US warehouse in Ohio. Standard US delivery is 3–5 business days. Free on orders over $50.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we ship only within the United States, including Alaska and Hawaii. International expansion is planned for late 2026.

How do I track my order?

You'll receive a tracking link by email the moment your order ships. You can also track from your account under Orders.

What if my order is late or missing?

Email hello@lullpet.com with your order number. We reply within 12 hours. Lost shipments are fully replaced at no charge.

ii. Returns & refunds

The 30-day calm guarantee.

What's your return policy?

Thirty days from delivery. Full refund. No restocking fees. Items only need to be in reasonable condition — we understand pets test things. Email hello@lullpet.com to start.

Do I pay return shipping?

For defective or damaged items, we cover it. For change-of-mind returns, the customer covers return shipping (around $5–8 via USPS).

When will I get my refund?

Refunds process within 3 business days of us receiving your return. You'll see the credit on your original payment method in 5–10 business days.

iii. Products & use

Built for real pets.

Are your products safe for puppies and kittens?

Yes — every product is rated for all ages unless explicitly noted. For pets under 3 months, check the specific product's care guide.

How long until I see results from calming products?

Most pets show calmer behavior within 3–7 days. Rescue pets or pets with chronic anxiety may take 2–4 weeks. Be patient — calm is built, not given.

Is the Calming Cuddler Bed machine washable?

Yes. Cold water, gentle cycle, air dry. It holds its plush shape for 50+ washes.

Are the pheromone diffusers safe around babies and other pets?

Yes. Pheromones are species-specific — humans, dogs, and birds can't detect them. Cats only respond to feline pheromones. They are 100% drug-free.

What's in your calming spray?

A blend of lavender essential oil, chamomile extract, and purified water. No alcohol, no parabens, no synthetic fragrance. Vet-formulated.

iv. About Lullpet

The brand behind it.

Where are you based?

Brooklyn, NY for our founder office, with fulfillment in Ohio. All customer service is US-based.

Is Lullpet a small business?

Yes. We're founder-owned, a team of six. Every email gets a reply from a real person.

Do you offer subscribe & save?

Yes — on consumables like sprays and diffuser refills, save 15% with monthly auto-ship. Pause or cancel anytime in your account.

How does the 1% to shelters work?

One percent of every order's revenue — not profit — is set aside monthly and donated to a rotating roster of vetted shelters. We publish a full impact report each January.

Can I gift a Lullpet product?

Absolutely. Add a gift note at checkout. We can include a hand-written card on request — just reply to your order confirmation email.
