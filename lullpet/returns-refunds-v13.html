The details

Small print, spelled plainly.

Return shipping

Defective or damaged on arrival: we cover the return shipping entirely.

Change of mind: the customer covers return shipping, around $5–8 via USPS.

Refund timing

Once we receive your return:

  • Processed within 1–3 business days
  • Visible on your card in 5–10 business days

You'll get an email confirmation at every step.

Condition

Items don't need to be in original packaging or unused — just in reasonable condition. We know pets test things, and we don't penalize you for it.

Exceptions

For hygiene reasons, opened pheromone refills and used grooming products can't be returned. If an order arrives damaged, send a photo — we'll replace it without needing the return.
