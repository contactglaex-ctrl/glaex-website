The basics

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites remember you, your preferences, and your activity — making sites faster, more useful, and more relevant.

Most cookies expire after a session or a set number of days. You can delete them anytime from your browser settings.

What we use

Four kinds of cookies, nothing else.

Essential
Site function

Required for the site to work — cart, checkout, login, secure session. Can't be disabled without breaking core features.

Analytics
How we improve

Help us understand how visitors use our site, anonymously. We use Google Analytics 4 with IP anonymization enabled.

Marketing
Relevant ads

Let us show you Lullpet ads on Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, and Google. We use Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, and Google Ads tag.

Preferences
Your settings

Remember your currency, language, recently viewed products, and whether you've dismissed the newsletter popup.

Your choices

You're in control.

When you first visit our site, a cookie banner asks for your consent. You can:

  • Accept all cookies (recommended for full experience)
  • Customize your preferences via the cookie banner
  • Disable cookies entirely in your browser (some features may not work)
  • Withdraw consent anytime by clicking "Cookie settings" in the footer

For the full picture on how we handle personal data, read our Privacy Policy.
