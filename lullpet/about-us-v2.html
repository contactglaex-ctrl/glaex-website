How it began

It started on a Tuesday night in Brooklyn.

My rescue, Maya, had been pacing the apartment for three hours. She'd done it every night since I adopted her — pacing, panting, never settling. I tried everything I could find: training, supplements, even prescriptions. Nothing fully worked.

I went looking for help — really looking — and what I found was a market flooded with cheap, generic "calming" products that were mostly marketing. Hollow promises wrapped in fluffy faux fur.

So I started building what I wished existed.

A short timeline

From one anxious dog to four thousand calm ones.

2023

Maya enters the story

Sarah adopts Maya, a four-year-old rescue with severe separation anxiety. Months of trial and error begin — training, supplements, medication, none of it fully works.

2024

The first prototype

After a year of research and three veterinary consultations, the first version of the Calming Cuddler Bed is built and tested with twelve rescue pets in Brooklyn. The results are immediate.

2025

Lullpet launches

The brand opens to the public with three core products and a single promise: vet-approved or it doesn't ship. The first 1,000 orders sell out in eleven weeks.

2026

Four thousand calm pets

Lullpet now serves pet parents across all fifty US states. $12,400+ donated to shelters. A growing line of calming essentials, all backed by the same thirty-day guarantee.

What we believe

A small set of quiet principles.

i

Science over marketing

If we can't point to research or a vet endorsement, we don't make the claim. The pet wellness market needs less noise — not more.

ii

Calm is built, not given

The best products work with your pet, not on them. Real calm takes consistency — a routine, a den, a sense of safety. We design for that.

iii

Honesty over hype

We don't say "100% guaranteed" or "miracle results." We say what we can prove — and we publish our impact, our sustainability work, and our limitations every January.

iv

Quiet is a luxury

Modern life is loud. Anxious pets sense that. We make products that bring stillness — beautiful enough to leave out, soft enough to sink into.
