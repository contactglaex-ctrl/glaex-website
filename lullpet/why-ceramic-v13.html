Why ceramic beats plastic for pet fountains

Material matters more than most pet parents realize. Here's the honest, side-by-side comparison — and why we chose ceramic.

The problem with plastic

Most pet fountains are plastic. That's the issue.

Plastic is cheap to manufacture, which is why the pet aisle is full of it. But for something your cat or dog drinks from every single day, plastic carries real downsides — and they get worse with time.

As plastic ages, its surface develops microscopic scratches. Those scratches trap bacteria and biofilm that ordinary rinsing can't reach. Plastic also absorbs odors, and many cats — far more scent-sensitive than humans — simply stop drinking from a bowl that smells "off" to them.

There's also feline chin acne: a common skin condition vets frequently link to bacteria harbored in plastic bowls and fountains.

"If your pet avoids their fountain, the material may be the reason — not the water."

Ceramic solves the problems at their root. It's non-porous, naturally bacteria-resistant, odor-free, and easy to keep genuinely clean. That's why we built the Lullpet fountain in ceramic — not plastic.

Common questions

Ceramic, answered.

Is ceramic better than plastic for pet fountains?

Yes. Ceramic is non-porous, so it resists the bacteria and biofilm that build up in scratched plastic. It holds no odor, stays cooler, is easy to deep-clean, and is heavy enough not to tip. For a vessel your pet drinks from daily, ceramic is the healthier choice on every measure that matters.

Why do some cats stop drinking from plastic bowls?

Cats have a far sharper sense of smell than humans. Plastic absorbs odors over time, and a bowl that smells faintly of detergent, old water, or plastic itself can be enough for a sensitive cat to refuse it. Ceramic stays completely odor-neutral.

Is the Lullpet ceramic fountain hard to clean?

No — it's easier than plastic. The ceramic top is dishwasher-safe, and the smooth, non-porous surface doesn't develop the sticky film plastic does. Rinse daily, deep-clean weekly, and replace the filter monthly.

Is ceramic safe if my pet knocks it?

Our ceramic is fired to be durable and chip-resistant for everyday use. Its weight is actually a safety advantage — it stays grounded and won't tip or slide the way lightweight plastic fountains do.

What about stainless steel?

Stainless steel is a solid second choice — hygienic and durable. We chose ceramic because it stays naturally cooler, never carries a metallic taste, and looks calm and premium in a home rather than industrial.
