The details

Small print, spelled plainly.

Return shipping

Defective or damaged on arrival: we cover it.

Change of mind: customer covers return shipping (~$5–8 via USPS).

Refund timing

Once we receive your return:

  • Refund processed: 1–3 business days
  • Visible on your card: 5–10 business days (depends on your bank)

You'll get an email confirmation at every step.

Exceptions

For hygiene reasons, the following cannot be returned:

  • Opened pheromone refills
  • Used grooming products

If your order was damaged in transit, send a photo — we'll send a replacement without needing the return.

Condition

Items don't need to be in original packaging or unused — just in reasonable condition. We know pets test things.
