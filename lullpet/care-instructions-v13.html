Care Instructions

Made to last. Cared for simply.

A little care keeps every Lullpet product soft, safe, and effective for years. Here's how, product by product.

Calming Cuddler Bed

Faux fur · machine washable
i

Machine wash cold

Use a gentle cycle with mild, fragrance-free detergent. Wash separately the first time.

ii

Air dry only

Lay flat or hang to dry. Avoid the dryer — high heat can flatten the faux fur over time.

iii

Fluff after drying

Once dry, give the rim a gentle shake and fluff to restore its full, cozy shape.

iv

Wash every 2–3 weeks

Regular washing keeps it hygienic and helps your pet associate it with comfort.

Ceramic Pet Fountain

Ceramic · dishwasher-safe top
i

Rinse daily

Empty and rinse the bowl each day to keep water fresh and inviting.

ii

Deep clean weekly

Disassemble fully and wash with warm soapy water. The ceramic top is dishwasher-safe.

iii

Replace filter monthly

Swap the charcoal filter every 3–4 weeks for clean, oxygenated water.

iv

Clean the pump

Rinse the pump and clear the impeller monthly to keep it whisper-quiet.

Calming Spray & Diffuser

Store cool · keep upright
i

Store away from heat

Keep the spray and diffuser refills in a cool, dark place to preserve the formula.

ii

Shake before use

Give the calming spray a gentle shake to evenly blend the natural ingredients.

iii

Keep the diffuser upright

Always plug in vertically so the refill flows correctly and lasts the full 30 days.

iv

Wipe the diffuser unit

Before a new refill, wipe the plug-in unit with a dry cloth — never submerge it.

Good habits

Three rules for everything.

Skip harsh chemicals

Avoid bleach and strong fragrances — they can irritate sensitive pets and break down materials.

Inspect regularly

Check for loose seams or worn parts every few months. Email us if anything needs attention.

Let it dry fully

Always allow washed items to dry completely before returning them to your pet.
