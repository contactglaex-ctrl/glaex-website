How it began

It started on a Tuesday night in Brooklyn.

My rescue, Maya, had been pacing the apartment for three hours. She'd done it every night since I adopted her — pacing, panting, never settling. I tried everything: training, supplements, even prescriptions. Nothing fully worked.

I went looking for help — really looking — and what I found was a market flooded with cheap, generic "calming" products that were mostly marketing. Hollow promises wrapped in fluffy faux fur.

So I started building what I wished existed.

Lullpet is calming essentials for cats and dogs, designed with veterinary input and tested in real homes with real anxious pets. No fillers. No empty claims. Just thoughtful products that help pets rest.

Sarah Mitchell Founder & CEO · Brooklyn, NY

What we believe

A quiet set of principles.

i

Science over marketing

If we can't point to research or vet endorsement, we don't make the claim. Less noise, more proof.

ii

Calm is built, not given

The best products work with your pet, not on them. Real calm takes consistency — a routine, a den, safety.

iii

Honesty over hype

No "100% guaranteed" or "miracle results." We say what we can prove, and publish our limitations annually.

iv

Quiet is a luxury

Modern life is loud. Anxious pets feel that. We make products that bring stillness — beautiful and soft.
