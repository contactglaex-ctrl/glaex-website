i. Shipping & delivery

How we get to your door.

How fast do you ship?

Orders ship within 24 hours from our US warehouse in Ohio. Standard US delivery is 3–5 business days. Free on orders $50+.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we ship only within the United States, including Alaska and Hawaii. International expansion is planned for late 2026.

How do I track my order?

You'll receive a tracking link via email the moment your order ships. You can also track from Account → Orders, or from our tracking page.

What if my order is late or missing?

Email hello@lullpet.com with your order number. We reply within 12 hours. Lost shipments are fully replaced, no charge.

ii. Returns & refunds

The 30-day calm guarantee.

What's your return policy?

30 days from delivery. Full refund. No restocking fees. Items must be in good condition (we understand pets test things). Email hello@lullpet.com to start a return.

Do I pay return shipping?

For defective or damaged items: we cover it. For change-of-mind returns: customer covers return shipping (~$5–8 via USPS).

When will I get my refund?

Refunds process within 3 business days of us receiving the return. You'll see the credit on your original payment method in 5–10 business days.

iii. Products & use

Built for real pets.

Are your products safe for puppies and kittens?

Yes — every product is rated for all ages unless explicitly noted. For pets under 3 months, check the specific product's care guide.

How long until I see results from calming products?

Most pets show calmer behavior within 3–7 days. Rescue pets or pets with chronic anxiety may take 2–4 weeks. Be patient — calm is built, not given.

Is the Calming Cuddler Bed machine washable?

Yes. Cold water, gentle cycle, air dry. Holds plush after 50+ washes.

Are the pheromone diffusers safe around babies and other pets?

Yes. Pheromones are species-specific — humans, dogs, and birds can't detect them. Cats only respond to feline pheromones. 100% drug-free.

What's in your calming spray?

A blend of lavender essential oil, chamomile extract, and purified water. No alcohol, no parabens, no synthetic fragrance. Vet-formulated.
