Our Promise

Calm pets. Better world.

1% of every order goes to vetted animal shelters and rescues across the United States. Not "up to". Not "sometimes". Every single order, every single time.

Why one percent

Because the calm we sell at home should reach pets without one.

The pets in our homes are lucky. They have beds, names, dinner waiting. Most of the pets in shelters had that too, once — and lost it through no fault of their own.

We can't fix the whole system. But we can take a fixed percentage of every order we ship and direct it, transparently and consistently, to the people working on the ground.

"Not a marketing line. A line item on every invoice."

Every order placed on lullpet.com sets aside 1% of revenue — not profit — for shelter and rescue work. We donate monthly. We publish annually. And we never quietly stop.
