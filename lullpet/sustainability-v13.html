Sustainability

Calm pets need a calm planet.

We don't claim to be perfectly sustainable. We claim to be honest about what we're doing — and what we're still working on.

Where we stand

Honest, not performative.

Sustainability has become a marketing checkbox for most pet brands — green leaf icons, vague "eco" language, recyclable claims with no infrastructure behind them. We try to do less of that.

"Sustainability is a slow, deliberate practice we get better at year by year."

Below is what we actually do today — and what we're actively working on. Read it. Hold us to it. We publish a full progress report every January.

What we do today

Five things that aren't claims.

✓

Recyclable packaging

Soy ink, no plastic windows, FSC-certified paper. Every box breaks down for curbside recycling.

✓

Carbon-neutral shipping

We offset the emissions of every order automatically, at no cost to you, through a verified partner.

✓

Sustainable materials

Faux fur instead of real. OEKO-TEX certified textiles. BPA-free components. No PFAS or fluorocarbons.

✓

US-based fulfillment

Our warehouse is in Ohio. Fewer transcontinental flights means a lower carbon footprint per shipment.

✓

Refill program

Buy refills instead of new diffusers and sprays — saving 60% on packaging waste and 20% on cost.

Our materials

What goes into every product.

Beds

Faux fur (vegan, plush, non-shedding) outer. Self-warming recycled PP cotton fill. Anti-slip cotton blend base.

Fountains

Premium ceramic, fully recyclable. BPA-free pump components. Triple-stage filtration: charcoal, foam, cotton.

Sprays

Glass bottles. Aluminum caps. Biodegradable formulas with lavender oil and chamomile extract.

Packaging

100% recycled cardboard. Soy-based ink. Zero plastic film. Hand-numbered thank-you cards on FSC paper.
