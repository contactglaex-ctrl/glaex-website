Our commitment

Designed to WCAG 2.1 AA.

Lullpet's website is designed to conform to Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards. We work with accessibility specialists to audit our site quarterly and address gaps as they're found.

Features we've implemented

  • Keyboard-friendly navigation throughout the site
  • Screen reader optimization with semantic HTML
  • Sufficient color contrast (WCAG AA minimum 4.5:1)
  • Descriptive alt text on all product and content images
  • Resizable text up to 200% without loss of function
  • Skip-to-content links on every page
  • ARIA labels on interactive elements (buttons, forms, accordions)
  • Focus-visible indicators on all interactive elements
  • Captions and transcripts on key video content

Third-party content

Where we depend on others.

Some third-party content on our site (embedded reviews, social media feeds, certain payment widgets) may not fully meet our accessibility standards. We're actively working with our partners to improve this and prioritize accessible vendors when we choose new tools.
