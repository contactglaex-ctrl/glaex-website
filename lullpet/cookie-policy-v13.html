Cookie Policy

Last updated · May 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how Lullpet uses them on lullpet.com, and the choices you have. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

Section 01What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites remember your actions and preferences — such as the items in your cart — and allow the site to function, improve, and personalize your experience. Similar technologies include pixels, tags, and local storage.

Section 02The types of cookies we use

TypeWhat it does
Essential Required for the site to function — shopping cart, secure checkout, login, and session security. These cannot be switched off.
Analytics Help us understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it. We use Google Analytics with IP anonymization enabled.
Marketing Let us show you relevant Lullpet ads on platforms such as Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, and Google, and measure their performance.
Preferences Remember choices such as your region, recently viewed products, and whether you have dismissed the newsletter pop-up.

Section 03Third-party cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services we use, including Shopify (our store platform), Google (analytics and ads), Meta (advertising), and our email and reviews providers. These third parties may use cookies to collect information about your activity across different websites.

Section 04Your choices

When you first visit our site, a cookie banner lets you accept or customize your preferences. You can also:

  • Accept all cookies for the full experience
  • Customize which non-essential categories you allow
  • Withdraw or change consent anytime via the "Cookie settings" link in our footer
  • Block or delete cookies through your browser settings — note that some features may not work correctly without them

Section 05Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers let you view, manage, and delete cookies through their settings menu. Refer to the help documentation for Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge for specific instructions. You can also opt out of certain advertising cookies through industry tools such as the Digital Advertising Alliance.

Section 06Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our practices or applicable laws change. The current version will always be posted here with a revised "last updated" date.
