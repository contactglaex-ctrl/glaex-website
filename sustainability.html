Our Approach

Honesty over greenwashing.

The fashion industry has a sustainability problem. We're not pretending we've solved it. What we are doing is making considered, measurable progress — and being honest about where we still have work to do.

GLAEX was built on a simple premise: fewer, better pieces. Our entire business model — curation over catalog bloat, weekly drops in small quantities, no aggressive markdowns — is designed to reduce overproduction, the single biggest source of fashion waste.

We work with U.S.-based fulfillment partners to cut shipping emissions. We choose fabrics that last beyond a season. We refuse to chase trends that will be landfill by spring. And every year, we publish an honest progress report — not a marketing campaign.

Our Pillars

Six commitments.

Real action, measured outcomes, and the discipline to say what we don't do — not just what we do.

i.

No Overproduction

Small-batch drops, sized to expected demand. No mass clearance fires, no incineration of unsold stock — ever.

ii.

Considered Materials

We prioritize linen, cotton, modal, and TENCEL™ where possible. We disclose fabric composition on every product page.

iii.

U.S.-Based Logistics

85% of our inventory ships from U.S. warehouses, reducing transport emissions and supporting domestic supply chains.

iv.

Recyclable Packaging

All mailers, hangtags, and ribbons are recyclable or compostable. We use FSC-certified paper for everything printed.

v.

Transparent Pricing

We don't fake-discount. No phony "70% off" sales. Our prices reflect real cost — published clearly, never inflated.

vi.

Annual Impact Report

Every January, we publish a public report on our emissions, materials, and waste — with the wins and the misses.

Real Talk

We don't call ourselves "sustainable".

"The most sustainable garment is the one already in her closet. We're not here to convince her to buy more — we're here to make sure that when she does, what she buys earns its place and stays there."

"Sustainable" has become one of the most overused words in fashion. We try not to use it. Instead, we use words that mean something specific: smaller, slower, considered, traceable, measurable, accountable.

We're not certified anything. We're not perfect anything. We're a small American boutique trying to do better than the industry standard — and being transparent about where we still fall short.

Our Process

How we source.

Four checkpoints every piece passes through before it reaches her closet.

Source

We work with U.S.-based wholesale partners and LA-based fashion houses. We disclose origin on every product page.

Vet

Suppliers must meet our fabric, fit, and labor standards. We reject 60% of styles we sample — quality over quantity.

Curate

30-40 pieces per drop. We forecast demand using past data to avoid producing more than we'll sell.

Track

Every order is logged, every return analyzed. Patterns inform what we re-stock, what we retire, what we redesign.

Our Materials

What we prioritize.

The fabrics, finishes, and practices we lean into — and the ones we work to leave behind.

✓

Natural Fibers

Linen, cotton, and silk lead our material mix. We disclose every blend percentage on the product page.

✓

TENCEL™ & Modal

Wood-pulp-based fibers that biodegrade naturally. Cool, breathable, and softer than synthetic blends.

✓

OEKO-TEX® Certified Suppliers

Wherever possible, we partner with suppliers certified for low chemical use in the dyeing process.

✓

Recyclable Packaging

FSC-certified paper, recyclable mailers, plastic-free hangtags. Compostable when possible.

✓

Reduced Polyester

We minimize synthetic content. When used, it's recycled polyester or rPET — never virgin polyester.

✓

Carbon-Neutral Shipping

We offset 100% of domestic shipping emissions through our logistics partners' carbon programs.
