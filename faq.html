Orders & Payment

About your order.

How do I place an order?

Add items to your cart, click the cart icon, and follow the checkout process. You can check out as a guest or create an account for faster future orders and order tracking.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna, and Afterpay for buy-now-pay-later.

Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?

Orders are sent to our warehouse within minutes. Email hello@glaex.com immediately — if it hasn't shipped yet, we'll do our best to modify or cancel. Once shipped, you'll need to return it.

Will I receive an order confirmation?

Yes. You'll receive an email confirmation within 5 minutes of placing your order. If you don't see it, check your spam folder or contact us.

Can I use multiple discount codes?

Only one discount code can be applied per order. Sale items and bundles already include savings and may not be combined with additional codes.

Shipping & Delivery

Getting it to her.

How long does shipping take?

Standard U.S. shipping: 3-5 business days. Expedited: 2-3 days. Express: 1-2 days. Add 1-2 days for processing. International varies by country — see our Shipping Policy for full details.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes — free standard ground shipping on all U.S. orders over $75.

Do you ship internationally?

We currently ship to Canada, Mexico, the UK, Australia, and select EU countries. International customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes.

How do I track my order?

You'll receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships. You can track via the carrier (USPS, UPS, FedEx) or your GLAEX account.

My tracking says delivered but I didn't receive it. What now?

First, check with neighbors and household members. Packages occasionally scan as delivered 24 hours early. If still missing after 48 hours, contact us at hello@glaex.com and we'll help file a claim.

Returns & Refunds

If it's not quite right.

What's your return policy?

14 days from delivery to return unworn, unwashed items with original tags. Free U.S. return label for most orders. Full details at Returns & Refunds.

How do I start a return?

Visit glaex.com/returns, enter your order number and email, and follow the prompts. You'll get a prepaid USPS label within minutes.

When will I receive my refund?

Once we receive your return, refunds are processed within 5 business days to your original payment method. Bank processing may take an additional 3-10 days.

What items can't be returned?

Items 40%+ off, intimates, bodysuits, swimwear (if liner removed), earrings, beauty, gift cards, and anything marked "Final Sale."

Do you offer exchanges?

We process exchanges as a return + new order. Start a return for the original item and place a new order for the replacement — this ensures the new item ships immediately.

Products & Sizing

About the pieces.

How does GLAEX sizing run?

Most pieces run true to size. Specific fit notes (e.g., "runs small," "oversized") are included on every product page. See our Size Guide for measurements.

What size models are pictured?

Each product page lists the model's height and the size she's wearing. We photograph on multiple body types where possible.

Where are GLAEX products made?

We source from U.S.-based wholesale partners and L.A.-based boutique manufacturers. Country of origin is disclosed on every product page and care label.

How should I care for my GLAEX pieces?

Follow the care label inside each garment. Most pieces are machine washable on cold, gentle cycle. Hang dry to preserve shape and color. Wash inside out for darks and prints.

Do you restock sold-out items?

Sometimes — especially bestsellers. Use the "Notify Me" button on the product page to get an email when your size restocks.

Account & Communication

Your GLAEX account.

Do I need an account to shop?

No — you can check out as a guest. But creating an account lets you track orders, save favorites, and check out faster.

How do I subscribe to or unsubscribe from emails?

Subscribe at the footer of any page. Unsubscribe via the link at the bottom of any email — takes effect within 24 hours.

How do I opt into SMS messages?

At checkout or via our SMS signup form. Text STOP to any message to unsubscribe. Standard messaging rates may apply.

I forgot my password. What now?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. You'll receive a reset link by email within 2 minutes.

Do you offer student or military discounts?

Yes. We offer 15% off for verified students (via Student Beans) and active or veteran military (via ID.me). The verification widget appears at checkout.
