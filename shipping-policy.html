Processing Times

Orders placed Monday-Friday before 12pm CST ship the same business day. Orders placed after 12pm CST, on weekends, or on U.S. holidays ship the next business day. Most orders are processed within 1-2 business days.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can track in real time via your shipping carrier (USPS, UPS, or FedEx). Tracking updates every 24-48 hours.

If you haven't received tracking within 3 business days of ordering, email us at hello@glaex.com.

Address Accuracy

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. We are unable to reroute or modify orders after they ship. Orders returned due to incorrect addresses are subject to a $9.95 reshipment fee.

Lost or Stolen Packages

If your tracking shows "Delivered" but the package isn't there, please:

  • Check with neighbors, household members, and your front office (if applicable).
  • Wait 24-48 hours — packages occasionally scan early.
  • File a claim with the carrier directly.
  • Contact us at hello@glaex.com for help.

We offer Route package protection at checkout for $1.50 — recommended for high-value orders or apartment buildings.

Damaged or Missing Items

If your order arrives damaged or with missing items, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos at hello@glaex.com. We'll make it right — replacement, store credit, or refund.

Holidays & Peak Seasons

During major holidays (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Christmas) and peak seasons, processing and delivery may take 1-3 additional business days. Order early for time-sensitive purchases.

P.O. Boxes & APO/FPO

We ship to U.S. P.O. boxes via USPS only. APO/FPO addresses are supported at standard shipping rates.
