Why We're Covered

We don't chase press.
We earn it.

"We've never paid for a press placement. We've never hired a PR firm to plant a story. The coverage we receive comes from journalists who've discovered the brand the same way our customers do — by accident, and then on purpose."

If you're a journalist, podcaster, or editor working on a story about modern fashion, women-led brands, slow-luxury commerce, or the future of American boutiques — we'd love to talk. We respond to every press inquiry, usually within 24 hours.
