GLAEX is committed to making our website usable, navigable, and enjoyable for every customer — including those with disabilities and assistive technology needs.

Our Commitment

A site that works for her.

At GLAEX, we believe that great fashion — and the experience of shopping for it — should be accessible to every woman. Independently of how she navigates the web, the device she uses, or the assistive technology she relies on.

We are committed to providing a digital experience that conforms to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA — the international standard set by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). Our team works continuously to identify and remediate accessibility issues across our site, app, email communications, and customer service touchpoints.

We see accessibility not as a one-time project, but as an ongoing practice woven into how we design, build, and operate this brand.

Features

What we've built in.

Accessibility features available across the GLAEX experience right now.

✓

Keyboard Navigation

Every page is fully navigable with a keyboard alone — Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, and Space — without requiring a mouse or touchscreen.

✓

Screen Reader Support

Compatible with major screen readers: NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, and TalkBack. All images include descriptive alt text.

✓

Color Contrast Standards

All text and interactive elements meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 AA color contrast ratios for readability.

✓

Resizable Text

Text can be resized up to 200% without breaking layouts, loss of content, or loss of functionality.

✓

Clear Focus Indicators

Visible focus states on every interactive element so keyboard users always know where they are on the page.

✓

Descriptive Link Text

Links are written to make sense out of context — no generic "click here" or "read more" without surrounding meaning.

✓

Form Labels & Error Messages

Every form field has a clear, programmatically-associated label. Errors are announced clearly with guidance to fix them.

✓

Skip-to-Content Links

Skip-navigation links allow keyboard and screen reader users to bypass repetitive menus and jump to main content.

✓

Reduced Motion Support

We respect the prefers-reduced-motion setting in your browser — animations are minimized for users who request it.

✓

Multiple Payment & Checkout Options

Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna, and Afterpay — multiple paths through checkout to suit different needs.

Why It Matters

Accessibility is not optional.

"Designing for accessibility isn't a compliance checklist. It's a sign of respect — for the women who shop with us, for the diversity of how they experience the web, and for the principle that good design works for everyone."

Beautiful design and accessible design are not opposites — they're the same discipline taken seriously. Every accessibility decision we make also improves the experience for customers without disabilities: clearer labels, faster pages, simpler flows, more legible text.

When the site works better for someone using a screen reader, it works better for everyone.

Standards

What we follow.

The international guidelines and legal frameworks that shape our accessibility practice.

i.

WCAG 2.1 Level AA

Our site is designed to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.1 at Level AA — the international standard developed by the W3C.

ii.

ADA Title III

We follow the principles of the Americans with Disabilities Act for digital experiences, ensuring our online services remain available to all U.S. customers.

iii.

Section 508

We align our practices with Section 508 of the U.S. Rehabilitation Act, the standard for federal electronic and information technology accessibility.

Our Process

An ongoing practice.

Accessibility is not a feature we shipped once. It's a continuous, four-step cycle built into how we operate.

Audit

We conduct regular accessibility audits with both automated tools and manual testing by experienced reviewers.

Remediate

Identified issues are prioritized and resolved on a rolling basis — critical fixes within 72 hours where possible.

Train

Our design, engineering, and content teams receive ongoing training on accessibility best practices.

Listen

We act on real feedback from real customers. Every accessibility report is reviewed personally and responded to within 24 hours.
