How to Measure

Find your true size.

Grab a flexible tape measure (or use string + a ruler). Measure over thin clothing, not bare skin. Stand naturally — don't suck in or pose.

  • i.

    Bust

    Measure around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the tape level and snug but not tight.

  • ii.

    Waist

    Measure around the narrowest part of your waist — usually 1-2 inches above your belly button.

  • iii.

    Hip

    Measure around the fullest part of your hips, about 7-9 inches below your natural waist.

  • iv.

    Inseam

    Measure from the inside of your leg, from crotch to the bottom of your ankle bone.

Fit Notes

When in between sizes.

"Sizing isn't math. It's how the fabric, the cut, and your body all meet in real life. Use our fit notes on every product — they exist for exactly this reason."

Every product page includes a "Fit Note" from our team — written after we sample the piece on multiple body types. We'll tell you when something runs small, oversized, true to size, or somewhere in between.

i.

If you're between sizes

Going up usually wins for structured pieces (blazers, denim). Going down works for stretchy or oversized pieces.

ii.

If you measure differently in bust vs hip

Size to the largest measurement, then have minor alterations done if needed. For sets, size each piece separately when possible.

iii.

If sizing still feels off

Email hello@glaex.com with the product and your measurements. We'll recommend a size personally.
